What this list shows. Recurring contributions paid through eWAY that are due to be charged, but that the scheduled job will not charge because one of their contributions is Cancelled and is dated on or after the date the recurring contribution is waiting on. Cancelling a contribution makes the extension skip that installment and move the next scheduled date on, so a record appears here only if it was left that way before the upgrade, or if the contribution was cancelled in a way that did not trigger the skip. The list is empty when nothing is blocked.

What to do for each record. Click its Recurring ID to see the recurring contribution and its contributions, and find the cancelled one. Then choose:

  1. Carry on and skip that installment: press Reactivate and enter a Next Scheduled Date that is after the cancelled contribution's date (for example the date of the next installment). If you leave the date blank nothing changes and the record stays blocked. The record is charged when that date arrives.
  2. Collect that installment after all: set the cancelled contribution to Failed, then press Reactivate and enter today's date. The next run of the scheduled job charges it. Do this only after checking in the eWAY Business Centre that the donor was not already charged for it.
  3. Stop it for good: press Cancel. The recurring contribution is set to Cancelled and is never charged again. This does not contact eWAY.

A record leaves this list as soon as it is no longer blocked, cancelled or no longer In Progress. A warning in the system status check counts the records on this list.
